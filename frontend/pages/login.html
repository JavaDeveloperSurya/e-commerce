Authentication

Login with email OTP

Step 1
Enter your email first. After submit, a 6-digit OTP field will appear for verification.
This calls /api/auth/login on your backend.
Check your email inbox for the OTP.

What happens after verify?

  • Access token and refresh token are saved in localStorage.
  • Your JWT is decoded to identify the role.
  • You are redirected to the correct dashboard automatically.
